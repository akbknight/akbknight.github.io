Independent Project · Econometric Forecasting

U.S. Retail Demand Forecasting Engine

18-month forward retail sales projection with holdout backtesting

Problem & scope: Retail inventory planners and operations teams need reliable medium-term demand forecasts that account for macroeconomic trends, seasonality, and historical shocks rather than static linear extrapolations.

My contribution: Formulated state-space ETS(A,A,A) and Holt-Winters exponential smoothing models in Python using statsmodels. Integrated walk-forward holdout validation and built an interactive browser studio for backtesting.

Supported results: Achieved ~2.0% Mean Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) across 24-month walk-forward holdout backtesting, with analytical 80% and 95% prediction intervals evaluated against MAE ($10.8B) and RMSE ($13.2B) benchmark baselines.

Source provenance: Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED) monthly retail series RSXFS (Retail and Food Services, ex. Food Services), covering 320 monthly observations (1992–2024).
